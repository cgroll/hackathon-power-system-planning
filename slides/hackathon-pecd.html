Putting PECD v4.2 to work

Two challenges built on the Pan-European Climate Database (ENTSO-E) capacity factors — one checks the data against reality, one designs future systems.

Capacity factor = the share of a plant’s maximum rated output it actually delivers over time — near 0% when wind and sun are absent, 100% at full power.

Track B · Planning energy systems

Sizing renewables to survive the Dunkelflaute

PECD gives decades of hourly capacity factors for wind & PV across the EU. Size the build-out against the events that actually break the system — the multi-day lulls.

  • How much installed wind + PV do we need so supply meets demand through a Dunkelflaute — a prolonged low-wind, low-sun spell?
  • Size against the worst events, not the average: the binding constraint is the multi-day lull, not the annual mean.
  • Add battery flexibility: how much does storage shrink the required overbuild? Trade GW of capacity for GWh of storage.
  • Compare across the EU: wind-rich north vs solar-rich south, small vs large countries — the resilient mix differs.

The gap that must be filled. When combined wind + solar collapses for days, capacity, storage, or imports have to cover the shortfall.
